# Krafta — The Plan to 200 Merchants

*Last updated: 2026-06-30 · Goal: 200 paying merchants ≈ $5k MRR · Reads with [product-marketing.md](product-marketing.md), [pricing-and-packaging.md](pricing-and-packaging.md), [prospects-tashkent.md](prospects-tashkent.md)*

200 paying merchants at a ~$25 blended price (mix of Pro $20 + Business $39–49) = **~$5,000 MRR** — your North Star. This is a **~6–9 month** build, not a launch-week number. The plan is a repeatable acquisition *engine*, not a one-time push.

---

## 1. The funnel math (work backwards from 200)

| Stage | Definition | Rough conversion | Volume needed for 200 paying |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reached** | DM'd, visited, or saw content | — | ~3,000–4,000 touches |
| **Onboarded (live)** | Shop built, has a working storefront | ~15–20% of reached | ~1,000 shops live |
| **Activated** | Took ≥1 **real** order through Krafta | ~50% of onboarded | ~500 |
| **Paying** | On Pro or Business | ~40% of activated | **200** |

Two numbers decide everything:
- **Activation rate** (did they take a real order?) — the single best predictor of paying. A live shop that never takes an order churns. Obsess over time-to-first-order.
- **Free→paid %** — driven by the dine-in/delivery/Pay upgrade triggers in the packaging.

**Sales-led shortcut:** white-glove onboarding (you build their menu) converts far better than self-serve — closer to 40–50% onboarded→paid. For the first ~100 merchants, **do it by hand.** Automate later.

## 2. Channels, ranked for Tashkent

Ranked by leverage *for this market* — not the US-SaaS playbook (no cold email, no Apollo, no Google Ads first).

### Tier 1 — do these now (launch → first 100)

1. **Direct field + DM outreach (the engine).** The [prospect-list](prospects-tashkent.md) motion, made repeatable: a weekly cadence of Instagram/Telegram DMs + in-person visits to Tashkent cafes/shops. Fits Uzbek relationship-first business culture. *Field tip already found: Teplo / Benedict / Materia are all on Nukus st. — cluster visits by street.* **Target: ~50 DMs + 10–15 field visits/week.**
2. **Founding-merchant white-glove.** First 20–30 shops onboarded by hand → instant proof + word-of-mouth seeds. (See packaging doc.)
3. **Referrals & word-of-mouth.** Tashkent F&B is a tight community — owners know owners. **Give a free month for every merchant referred who goes live.** Ask every happy founding merchant for 2 intros. This is the cheapest, highest-trust channel here. *(`/referrals` skill.)*

### Tier 2 — switch on once you have proof (month 2+)

4. **"Powered by Krafta" loop.** Every storefront/QR a merchant shares carries a subtle *«Сделано на Krafta · Создать свою витрину»* link. Their customers include other business owners. Free, compounding distribution baked into the product. *(Build this — it's a code change, not a campaign.)*
5. **Instagram/Telegram content on real shops.** Short videos: *«собрали витрину кафе за вечер»*, before/after the order chaos. Show, don't tell. Needs proof first. *(`/social` skill.)*
6. **Partnerships / co-marketing.** POS & equipment resellers, food bloggers, Telegram entrepreneur/HoReCa communities, market & food-court organizers, coworking spaces. One partner can introduce dozens of merchants. *(`/co-marketing` skill.)*

### Tier 3 — later / opportunistic

7. **Tourist angle.** Partner with hotels, hostels, tourist-area venues on multilingual QR menus — a wedge competitors don't push. Ties to the RU/UZ/EN strength.
8. **Local launch & directories.** Local startup media, business chats, a "launch moment." Backlinks + awareness. *(`/launch`, `/directory-submissions`.)*
9. **"vs" / SEO pages.** The 5 angles in [competitors.md](competitors.md) (Krafta vs robo.uz, vs lacafe, the category roundup). Ranks in Google *and* arms sales. Build once you have proof.

> **Paid ads are deliberately last.** With CAC unknown, no proof, and an SMB audience that buys on trust and demos, paid spend is premature. Earn the first 100 by hand; let the data tell you if/when paid makes sense.

## 3. The sequence (90 days, then scale to 200)

**Week 1 — Launch & found.** Ship the public launch. Onboard **10–15 founding merchants** from the prospect list (white-glove). Ship two product loops: **referral reward** + **"powered by Krafta."** Instrument every shop for the **time-to-first-order** and **orders-processed** metrics.
→ *Exit: ~12 shops live, first orders flowing, proof-capture running.*

**Weeks 2–4 — Prove & systematize.** Turn outreach into a weekly cadence (50 DMs + 10 visits/wk). Publish the **first 2–3 case studies** (logo + quote + one hard number) the moment a founding merchant has results. Start asking for referrals.
→ *Exit: ~35–45 live, **first ~10–15 paying**, 3 case studies.*

**Month 2 — Content & referrals compound.** Content engine on real shops. Referral channel active. First partnership conversations. Build the top 2 "vs" pages now that proof exists.
→ *Exit: ~80–100 live, ~40–60 paying.*

**Month 3 — Widen channels.** Land 1–2 partnerships. Tourist/multilingual angle. Krafta Pay beta → start moving the dine-in segment from Pro → Business.
→ *Exit: ~120–140 live, ~70–90 paying.*

**Months 4–9 — Scale the engine.** Keep the outreach cadence, let referrals + content + powered-by compound, add channels as they prove out. Krafta Pay GA expands Business conversion + adds payment revenue on top of subscriptions.
→ *Exit: **~200 paying ≈ $5k MRR.***

## 4. The retention spine (don't let the bucket leak)

200 is a *net* number. At even 5%/mo logo churn you're refilling ~10 lost merchants every month — acquisition can't outrun a leaky product.

- **Time-to-first-order is the activation metric.** Snap-menu onboarding + "orders by tonight." A merchant who takes an order in week 1 stays; one who doesn't, churns.
- **The Telegram order-alert habit** is the retention hook — every order is a re-engagement ping. Protect it.
- **Weekly value:** a simple weekly "you took N orders / earned X" recap (Telegram) keeps Krafta top-of-mind and makes the subscription feel earned.
- **Churn-prevention** (save offers, dunning when Krafta Pay bills) — set up before you have a churn problem. *(`/churn-prevention` skill.)*

## 5. What to measure (weekly)

| Metric | Why |
|---|---|
| Shops onboarded / week | Top-of-funnel velocity |
| **% activated (took a real order)** | Best predictor of paying — the one to obsess over |
| Free → paid % | Packaging working? |
| Time-to-first-order (median) | Onboarding quality |
| Logo churn % | Is the bucket leaking? |
| MRR + blended price | Progress to $5k |
| CAC (by channel, even rough) | When can paid turn on? |

## 6. Budget reality (bootstrapped / pre-funding)

Assume ~$0–2K/mo marketing spend. This plan is **labor-led, not capital-led** — it runs on founder/SDR time (outreach, white-glove onboarding), not ad budget. The marketing-skills library + the agents do the work of a small team:
- `prospecting` → the lists · `cold-email`/`sales-enablement` → the pitch + DM scripts (Telegram-adapted) · `social` → content · `referrals` → the program · `competitors` → the vs-pages · `analytics` → the funnel instrumentation · `churn-prevention` → retention.

When the round closes or revenue funds it, the first paid experiments are Instagram/Telegram ads to lookalikes of activated merchants — *after* CAC is known.
