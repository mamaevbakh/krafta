# Competitive Intelligence — Uzbek QR-Menu / Ordering Platforms

*Compiled: 2026-06-30. For Krafta product marketing.*
*Method: live-rendered each site (gstack headless browser) + WebFetch + WebSearch. Marketing copy quoted verbatim with language noted. Source cited per claim.*

> **Headline takeaways**
> 1. **Nobody charges commission.** Every competitor with public pricing is a flat subscription (per-month or per-branch). robo.uz and laCafe both explicitly defend "subscription, not a cut." So Krafta's "infrastructure, not commission" line does NOT differentiate against these five — it differentiates against marketplaces (Uzum/Yandex), which is a different fight. **Adjust the pitch accordingly.**
> 2. **The real money band is set by laCafe ($100–300/mo) and robo.uz (300k–900k UZS/mo ≈ $24–72/mo).** Krafta's ~$20/mo Pro lands *below* both — that's a wedge, not a weakness.
> 3. **AI is essentially empty.** Only robo.uz lists "Artificial Intelligence" as a (vaguely-defined, tier-gated) feature row. None have conversational ordering, chat-to-order, or an AI waiter. **Krafta's conversational-commerce bet is unclaimed.**
> 4. **laCafe is the one to watch** — funded ($100K, United Ventures, Apr 2026), ~100 businesses, deepest delivery-ops + aggregator integrations. robo.uz is the broadest (retail + HoReCa, deep POS/payment/delivery integrations, real client logos).
> 5. **qr-menu.uz / menuqr.uz / restorica.uz are thin** — static QR-menu display, weak or no ordering, abandoned-looking. Easy to leapfrog.

## 1. robo.uz (Robosell) — BIGGEST / broadest

- **URLs:** robo.uz (marketing, RU/UZ/EN at /en) → robosell.uz (the app). Operated by RoboSell.
- **Language:** UZ primary, full RU + EN. Tashkent.

**Positioning / tagline (EN site, verbatim):**
- *"Start your online business in just 15 minutes"*
- *"Become a new model of success in online business, managing everything on a single platform."*
- Self-description: *"A full-featured constructor for creating websites and Telegram bots, ideally suited for the HoReCa sector and any type of store looking to launch online sales."*
- Strong "build-vs-buy" framing: a table contrasting *custom development* (Telegram bot 5–7M, website 15–20M, total **50–62M sums**) vs *"Included in the subscription… 500,000 sums."*

**Pricing model — FLAT MONTHLY SUBSCRIPTION (UZS), tiered. No commission.** *(robo.uz/en tariffs, verbatim numbers)*
| Plan | Price/mo | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Start** | **300,000 UZS** (≈ $24) | Telegram bot (WebApp) + website (subdomain only) |
| **Basic** | **500,000 UZS** (≈ $40) | + full website, QR menu/catalog, Instagram |
| **Professional** | **900,000 UZS** (≈ $72) | + Click SuperApp |
- Term discounts: 6 months −10%, 12 months −20%. Free trial offered.
- Feature gates by tier: Start = 1 branch / 500 products / 300 orders; Basic = 5 branches / 3,000 products; Pro = unlimited.

**Core features:**
- Website + Telegram bot builder; Instagram channel; QR menu/catalog
- Two design modes: **Restaurant** and **Online store** (dual vertical)
- Orders, employees/roles/couriers, chat with customers, promo codes, discounts, advertising banners, search
- **Deep integrations** (the moat): Payments **Payme / Click / Uzum / Atmos**; Delivery **Fargo / UzPost / Yandex b2b**; **POS: iiko / R-Keeper / Poster**; Inventory: Billz / Smartup / Sales Doctor; IP telephony: Online PBX / Sipuni
- Analytics: revenue, orders, traffic source, top-10 products, order map, orders-by-time, top-10 customers
- Real client logos (MonaBek, Jumanji, N'Medov, Сыроварня, Lago Park, DI sport…) + named testimonials — **the only competitor with visible social proof.**

**AI features:** Lists **"Artificial intelligence"** as a feature row in the tariff comparison (tier-gated), but **no description of what it does** — no visible chat ordering, smart search, or recommendations. Effectively a checkbox, not a product.

**Target vertical:** Both **HoReCa AND retail/online stores** (explicit "Robo Restaurant" + "Robo Shop" product lines).

**Strength vs Krafta:** Broadest integration surface (POS/payments/delivery/inventory/telephony all live), real customer logos, dual vertical, aggressive build-vs-buy economic argument.
**Weakness vs Krafta:** Generic "constructor" UX (admin-panel heavy, looks like a builder, not a brand). No conversational/AI ordering of substance. Most expensive at the top end. Not Telegram-*native* in the Mini-App-storefront sense — Telegram is one of several output channels.

**Positioning / tagline (RU, verbatim):**
- *"Запустите доставку всего за 3 дня"* (Launch delivery in just 3 days)
- *"Полное решение для кафе и ресторанов для управления онлайн-заказами и доставкой — без хлопот и с ростом"* (A complete solution for cafes & restaurants to manage online orders and delivery — hassle-free and built to grow)
- Spot.uz description: *"универсальную программную платформу для ресторанов и кафе. Она объединяет онлайн-заказы, управление доставкой, связи с клиентами (CRM), программы лояльности и аналитику в единую систему."*
- Outcome stats on page: *"30% Увеличение прибыли от доставки · 20% Среднее увеличение чека · 2X Средний прирост перепродаж · Доверяют ~100 бизнесов."*

**Pricing model — FLAT SUBSCRIPTION, USD, PER-BRANCH. No commission.** *(lacafe.uz/ru/tariffs, verbatim)*

**Target vertical:** **Restaurants & cafes only.** Delivery-first.

**Strength vs Krafta:** Funded + real traction (~100 businesses); deepest delivery operations (own-courier dispatch + courier app + call center); aggregator order aggregation (Wolt/Uzum Tezkor/Yandex Eats in one inbox) — something Krafta doesn't do; native mobile apps; outcome-led marketing (30% / 20% / 2X) that mirrors Krafta's own voice.
**Weakness vs Krafta:** Restaurant-only (no retail/showroom/services). USD pricing is steep for a small single-venue cafe ($100/mo floor). "Получить демо" / consultant-led sales = slow, high-touch onboarding (vs Krafta's "taking orders by tonight, no signup"). No AI/conversational layer. No "free to start."

## Where Krafta WINS (gaps none of them fill)

1. **Conversational commerce / AI ordering — wide open.** Zero competitors have chat-to-order, an AI waiter, or smart natural-language search. robo.uz's lone "Artificial intelligence" checkbox is undefined and undescribed. This is Krafta's clearest white space.
2. **AI shop-builder (Krafta Studio).** "Describe your shop, AI builds a real coded storefront." Nobody else is within sight of this. It's a category-of-one differentiator if marketed.
3. **Free to start, no signup, instant draft.** Only qr-menu.uz has a free *trial*. laCafe is demo/consultant-led; robo.uz trial-only. Krafta's "draft with no signup, taking orders by tonight" is a genuinely lower-friction entry than all five.
4. **One storefront for every order mode (dine-in + pickup + delivery + browse).** robo.uz and laCafe are channel/delivery-centric; the others don't order at all. Krafta's "one menu, every order, yours" framing is cleaner.
5. **Telegram-*native storefront* (Mini App), not just a bot channel.** robo.uz/laCafe treat Telegram as one output; Krafta's storefront *runs inside* Telegram where Uzbek merchants already live.
6. **Genuine multi-vertical (retail/showroom/services), with restaurant-grade modifiers.** Only robo.uz also does retail; laCafe/qr-menu/restorica are food-only. Krafta serves shops AND has real-menu variations/modifiers.
7. **Lower price floor with a real product behind it.** Krafta Pro at ~$20/mo undercuts laCafe ($100) and robo.uz Pro ($72) while offering more than qr-menu.uz at a similar price.
8. **Design / craft bar.** restorica and qr-menu look dated; robo.uz looks like an admin builder. Krafta's Square/Vercel-grade craft is a visible, defensible edge for a brand-conscious owner.

## Where Krafta is at PARITY or BEHIND

1. **No published proof / customers (BEHIND).** robo.uz has logos + testimonials; laCafe has funding + ~100 businesses + outcome stats. Krafta has none public yet. **This is the #1 acquisition gap** — first named merchants + an order/hours stat are urgent.
2. **POS integration (BEHIND).** robo.uz and laCafe integrate iiko/R-Keeper/Poster (and Billz/Smartup). Krafta doesn't yet. For venues already on a POS, this is a real objection.
3. **Aggregator order aggregation (BEHIND vs laCafe).** laCafe pulls Wolt / Uzum Tezkor / Yandex Eats orders into one inbox. Krafta has no marketplace-intake play.
4. **Own-courier delivery ops (BEHIND vs laCafe).** laCafe ships courier app + dispatcher panel + call center. Krafta dispatches Yandex couriers but has no own-fleet management layer.
5. **Native mobile apps (PARITY-ish/BEHIND).** laCafe offers branded iOS/Android apps (paid). Krafta is web + Telegram Mini App (arguably the right bet for this market, but it's a checkbox laCafe can claim).
6. **Funding / momentum narrative (BEHIND vs laCafe).** laCafe can say "VC-backed." Krafta needs its own momentum story (Krafta Pay, Studio, first merchants).

## Pricing Read — what band should Krafta Pro sit in?

**Market anchors (monthly, single venue):**
- Floor: qr-menu.uz **~$9.5–17** (but it's a thin menu tool)
- Mid: robo.uz **~$24 (Start) → $40 (Basic) → $72 (Pro)**
- Premium: laCafe **$100 (Start) → $300 (Plus)**, per-branch

**Read:**
- The **functional ordering tier in this market clears at roughly $24–40/mo** (robo.uz Start/Basic — the closest like-for-like to Krafta's storefront+orders). laCafe at $100 is priced as a *delivery-operations suite for multi-branch chains*, not a single-cafe storefront — a different buyer.
- **Krafta Pro at ~$20/mo is well-positioned: it sits just under robo.uz's entry ($24) and far under laCafe**, while the free tier undercuts everyone on entry friction. That's a defensible "more product, less money" wedge — keep it.
- **Consider a two-tier Pro** to capture the gap up to robo.uz Basic/Pro ($40–72): e.g. **Pro ~$20/mo** (storefront + all order modes + Telegram + translations) and a **Business/Pro+ ~$39–49/mo** (delivery zones + Krafta Pay + analytics + AI ordering when it lands). This mirrors how both robo.uz and laCafe ladder features by tier and leaves room to monetize the AI/conversational layer as the premium hook.
- **Anchor on value, not the cheapest price.** Don't race qr-menu.uz to $9.5 — that frames Krafta as a menu tool. Anchor against robo.uz's own build-vs-buy math (50–62M sums custom dev) and laCafe's $100 floor to make ~$20 feel like a steal for a real system.
- **Per-branch is the market norm for multi-venue** (both laCafe and robo.uz gate by branches). Krafta can credibly add per-additional-location pricing later without surprising the market.
- **Krafta Pay (flat fee, not %) is the second monetization layer** and is consistent with the no-commission stance — none of these five monetize payments via commission either, so the differentiator there is again *vs marketplaces*, not vs these competitors.

## 3–5 "vs / alternative" page angles for SEO + sales

1. **"Krafta vs robo.uz (Robosell)"** — the broadest direct comparison. Angle: *storefront + brand + conversational ordering vs a generic site/bot constructor.* Win on craft, free-to-start, Telegram-native storefront, AI. Concede/neutralize POS by positioning Krafta as "your storefront and order system, not another admin panel." High-intent: people literally search "Robosell альтернатива."
2. **"Krafta vs lacafe.uz"** — the premium-competitor comparison. Angle: *taking orders by tonight, free to start, ~$20/mo vs $100/mo + per-branch and a consultant demo.* Position Krafta for the single venue / small operator that laCafe over-serves and over-charges. Be honest that laCafe is strong for big multi-branch delivery chains; Krafta wins the long tail.
3. **"Лучшие QR-меню и системы заказов для кафе в Узбекистане (2026)"** — a roundup/listicle ("best QR-menu & ordering platforms in Uzbekistan") that profiles robo.uz, laCafe, qr-menu.uz, restorica.uz and positions Krafta as the modern, AI-native, free-to-start option. Captures category search, earns the comparison framing, and is the natural hub linking out to the vs/ pages.
4. **"QR-меню — это не система. Почему витрина важнее меню"** (alternative angle, not a named competitor) — targets qr-menu.uz / restorica.uz searchers who think they want "just a QR menu," and reframes the need as *a system, not a menu* (Krafta's core narrative). Pulls low-end searchers up-market into Krafta.
5. **"Krafta vs маркетплейсы (Uzum, Yandex Eats): витрина без комиссии"** — the *real* commission fight. Since no direct competitor charges commission, the "infrastructure, not commission" message belongs on a page targeting merchants frustrated with Uzum Tezkor / Yandex Eats taking a cut and owning the customer. This is where Krafta's strongest line actually lands.

*(Optional 6th: "restorica.uz alternative" / "menuqr.uz alternative" — low-volume but trivially winnable since both are thin; bundle into the roundup rather than standalone pages.)*
