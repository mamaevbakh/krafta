# Tashkent SMB Prospect List — Krafta Launch Outreach

*Compiled: 2026-06-30*

**Purpose:** A ready-to-outreach hit-list of independent Tashkent cafes, coffee shops, restaurants, chaikhanas, and local retail shops to pitch during Krafta's launch week. ICP = small/independent (single-venue or a few branches), active digital presence (Instagram/Telegram, posts menu/products, takes orders in DMs), but **no proper online storefront or ordering system of their own** — the "order chaos" pain Krafta solves.

**Method & limits (read this):**
- Discovery via **Google Maps** category searches (`кофейня / чайхана / ресторан Ташкент`) + **WebSearch** (US-routed engine) cross-referenced against each business's Google listing, Instagram, Telegram, Facebook, and local directories (resto.uz, afisha.uz, 2gis, goldenpages).
- **Public contact channels only** — phones and Instagram handles came from Google Maps place pages, public Instagram bios, and public directory listings. No login-wall bypass, no bulk scraping, requests spaced.
- **Phones** are mostly from Google Maps place panels; verified present at lookup time but not dialled. **Instagram handles** confirmed to resolve to a real public profile via search; **not** opened behind Instagram's login wall, so follower counts / "orders in DM" behavior are inferred from public bios + directory descriptions, not first-hand.
- Where a contact channel could not be confirmed from public sources it is marked **"needs manual lookup."**
- **Disqualified during research (NOT in list):** Caffelito (83 branches), Yess Coffee (21 branches), Bon!/boncafe_uz (chain, 6 cities, own delivery), Safia (~60 branches + safiabakery.uz), Chaykof (own iOS app w/ cashback = already has ordering), Beanberry (beanberry.uz + Telegram order-bot), Сыроварня (Arkady Novikov / Family Garden franchise), Toshkent Restaurant (large landmark). These fail "small + independent" or "no ordering system yet."
- **Honest caveat:** This is assisted desktop research. Every name, phone, and handle below is a real business found in public sources (source URL captured per row). I did **not** place a test order, so the "takes orders in DMs" buying signal is inferred from each business being Instagram-primary with no checkout/storefront — strong but not field-confirmed. Confirm the live handle + phone before outreach (Instagram handles occasionally get rebranded).

## Top 5 to pitch first

1. **community.tashkent** — A ~5k-follower specialty-coffee brand running **two** small venues entirely through Instagram, with two reachable phone numbers. Multi-venue + brand-conscious + zero ordering system = a flagship "every order, yours" win and a future case study.
2. **Cafe Dosan** — Just opened its **2nd branch** (active expansion = budget + momentum) and is multilingual (KR/UZ/RU), so Krafta's RU/UZ/EN storefront lands hard. Greenfield: Instagram-only today.
3. **Book Cafe** — Sells food **plus** a retail market **plus** vinyl through one Instagram + a faceless resto.uz card. The multi-vertical (food + retail) story is exactly what differentiates Krafta from menu-only tools, and it's a vivid demo.
4. **Cvetochnaya Lavka (flowers)** — Pure DM-order-chaos: every bouquet ordered through Instagram DMs and a Telegram channel, citywide delivery, reachable phone. Retail proof that Krafta isn't just for cafes, and delivery+catalog map 1:1 to their daily pain.
5. **cake_decor.uz (custom cakes)** — Made-to-order cakes booked by DM with a 3-4 day lead and no calendar or checkout. The clearest single "order chaos" story on the list, and a satisfying before/after for a launch testimonial.

*(Honorable mention: Master Coffee and Benedict are equally Hot and Mirabad/central — easy to visit in person the same afternoon as the Nukus-st. cluster: Teplo Store, Benedict, and Materia all sit at Nukus 31/2-31/3, so one walk covers three prospects.)*

## What I could and couldn't verify

**Verified (High confidence):** Real business existence, category, district/address, a working Instagram handle that resolves to a public profile, and a phone number present on the Google Maps place panel — for rows 1-8, 10, 14, 15, 16 (the Hot cluster). Phones were read live off Maps; handles confirmed via search to be the real account (not a foreign namesake).

**Partially verified (Med confidence):** Rows 11, 17, 18, 19, 20 — handle confirmed, but the **phone is Instagram-DM-only** (Materia, hocolate, chocoway) or the listing leans on a directory (Zeyn's). Marked "needs manual lookup" where no public phone existed.

**Could NOT verify (Low / flagged):** **Space Coffee's Instagram handle** — its Maps listing carried no social link and every `spacecoffee_*` handle found belongs to a foreign city (Singapore/Almaty/Kyiv/Vienna). Phone is real; handle needs manual lookup on-site. **Chaykof** turned out to have its own ordering app, so it's a switch-pitch, not greenfield — kept but de-scored. **Arabica** has its own site (arabica.uz), so its no-storefront signal is weak — kept as an upgrade-pitch.

**The honest limitation:** The core buying signal — "takes orders in DMs/comments" — is **inferred**, not field-confirmed. I did not (and per the compliance brief should not) place test orders or open profiles behind Instagram's login wall. Every business here is Instagram-primary with no first-party checkout/storefront found in public sources, which is a strong proxy, but the founder/SDR should glance at each profile's recent posts before outreach to confirm DM-ordering and that the handle hasn't been rebranded. Follower counts cited come from search-snippet bios, not first-hand profile views.
