## Day by day

**Mon — Load & launch outreach.**
- Confirm each Hot handle/phone is live (some flagged "needs manual lookup"). Glance at recent posts to confirm they still take DM orders.
- Send **Touch 1** to all 9 DM prospects (personalized). Target: 9 sent.
- Prep the leave-behind card (QR → live demo) for the field walk.

**Tue — Field walk + first builds.**
- Walk Nukus st.: Teplo → Benedict → Warumkaffee → Materia. Snap menus, get Telegrams, leave cards.
- Any "yes" from Mon: **build their storefront tonight** (snap-menu → RU/UZ/EN → deliver link).

**Wed — Build & deliver.**
- Deliver finished storefronts to the first yeses; walk them through taking orders in Telegram.
- Send **Touch 2** (value) to Mon non-responders.
- Confirm founding status explicitly: *"функции Business по цене Pro закрепляются за вами навсегда."*

**Thu — Drive first orders.**
- For every live shop: get the QR on tables / link shared with regulars **today**. This is the activation push.
- Continue builds for Tue/Wed field yeses.

**Fri — Close the loop + measure.**
- Chase any warm-but-undecided (Touch 3 soft break-up on silent threads).
- Record: reached / replied / demo-sent / live / **first-order** for each. Update the tracker.
- Note median time-to-first-order — even from n=3, this is your first real data point.

**Sat/Sun — Overflow + reflect.**
- Finish any pending builds. Line up next week's 15 (warm list + referrals from happy founders).

## The white-glove build checklist (per "yes")

1. **Collect:** menu photos/PDF · logo (if any) · Telegram for orders · order types (dine-in / pickup / delivery) · address · phone.
2. **Build:** snap-menu extraction → storefront → translate RU/UZ/EN → publish.
3. **Deliver:** send the live link + a 2-line "how to receive orders in Telegram."
4. **Activate:** ask them to put the QR on tables / share the link with a few regulars **today**.
5. **Confirm founding status** in writing (Business-at-Pro, locked, in exchange for a case study later).

## Before you send anything (60-second rule)
- Open their recent posts; name a **specific** dish/product in the message.
- Match their language (RU default; switch to UZ if their content is UZ).
- Send during business hours; light emoji OK on IG, keep minimal.
- Stop the sequence the moment they reply — switch to a real conversation.

## The two things to instrument from shop #1
Even at n=1, start capturing: **(1) time-to-first-order** and **(2) orders processed**. These become both your activation metric and the hard number in your first case study — the asset that sells the next 30 merchants.
